## New Digital Platform Transforms Saudi Medical Licensing Exam Preparation

**Riyadh, Saudi Arabia — [DATE]** — SMLE Pro, a comprehensive digital preparation platform for the Saudi Medical Licensing Exam (SMLE), officially launches today, offering medical students and graduates an innovative way to master their licensing exam through AI-powered personalized learning.

### The Challenge

Every year, thousands of medical students and graduates in Saudi Arabia face the critical Saudi Medical Licensing Exam (SMLE), administered by the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS). Success on this exam is essential for medical practice in the Kingdom, yet many candidates struggle with:

- Limited access to high-quality, exam-style practice questions
- Lack of personalized feedback and progress tracking
- Insufficient study resources aligned with the official exam blueprint
- High cost of traditional prep courses

### The Solution

SMLE Pro addresses these challenges with a modern, mobile-first platform featuring:

- **Daily Dose**: 30 fresh, blueprint-aligned MCQs delivered daily at no cost
- **Instant Rationales**: Detailed explanations for every question
- **Performance Analytics**: Real-time tracking of strengths and weaknesses
- **Specialty Drills**: Focused practice in Surgery, Internal Medicine, Pediatrics, OB/GYN, and more
- **Timed Mock Exams**: Full-length simulations that replicate exam conditions
- **Bilingual Interface**: Seamless Arabic and English support

### Key Features

- **Free Tier**: No signup required for Daily Dose, ensuring accessibility for all students
- **Cultural Relevance**: Designed specifically for Saudi medical education context
- **Modern UX**: Glassmorphism design, mobile PWA, offline capabilities
- **Local Payments**: Integration with Moyasar for Mada, Visa, Apple Pay, and Samsung Pay

### Founder Statement

"Medical education in Saudi Arabia deserves world-class preparation tools," said Khaled Abdullah Ibrahim Al-Netaifi, founder of SMLE Pro. "We've built a platform that combines cutting-edge technology with deep understanding of the SMLE exam structure, making high-quality preparation accessible to every medical student in the Kingdom."
