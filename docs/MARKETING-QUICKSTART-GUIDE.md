## 📅 Day 6: Paid Advertising Test (1-2 hours)

### ✅ Set Up Small Test Campaigns

**Instagram/Facebook Ads:**
- Budget: 50 SAR/day for 3 days
- Target: Saudi Arabia, ages 22-30, interests: medicine, medical school
- Ad creative: "30 free SMLE questions daily" with app screenshot
- CTA: "Learn More" → website

**Google Ads:**
- Budget: 50 SAR/day for 3 days
- Keywords: "SMLE preparation", "امتحان الهيئة السعودية"
- Ad copy: "SMLE Pro - Free Daily Practice Questions"
- CTA: "Try Free" → website

### ✅ Track Results

**Metrics to Watch:**
- Click-through rate (CTR)
- Cost per click (CPC)
- Conversion rate (signups/clicks)
- Cost per acquisition (CPA)

## 📅 Day 7: Review & Plan Next Week (1 hour)

### ✅ Analyze Week 1 Performance

**Review:**
- [ ] Which social media posts got most engagement?
- [ ] How many new followers on each platform?
- [ ] How many email subscribers?
- [ ] How many website visitors?
- [ ] How many free users? How many paid?

**Calculate:**
- Engagement rate: (likes + comments + shares) / followers
- Conversion rate: (paid users / total users) × 100
- Cost per acquisition: ad spend / paid users

### ✅ Plan Week 2

Based on what worked:
- [ ] Double down on successful content types
- [ ] Adjust posting times based on engagement
- [ ] Refine ad targeting based on demographics
- [ ] Plan collaborations with engaged influencers
- [ ] Set specific goals for week 2

## 🚨 Common Pitfalls to Avoid

### ❌ Don't:
- Spam groups with promotional content
- Buy followers or engagement
- Ignore negative comments
- Post inconsistently
- Focus only on selling
- Expect immediate results

### ✅ Do:
- Provide genuine value first
- Engage authentically with community
- Respond to all feedback promptly
- Maintain consistent posting schedule
- Focus on helping, not just selling
- Be patient and persistent

1. **Consistency beats intensity** - Daily small actions > occasional big efforts
