## 🚨 Crisis Management Plan

### Potential Issues & Responses

#### Technical Problems
- **Issue:** App crashes or slow loading
- **Response:** Immediate technical team notification, transparent communication via social media, compensation offers

#### Negative Feedback
- **Issue:** Poor reviews or complaints
- **Response:** Personal response within 2 hours, move to private resolution, implement fixes quickly

#### Influencer Issues
- **Issue:** Influencer controversy or poor performance
- **Response:** Have backup influencers ready, monitor performance closely, adjust strategy as needed

#### Payment Problems
- **Issue:** Payment gateway failures
- **Response:** Clear communication about issue, alternative payment methods, refunds if needed
