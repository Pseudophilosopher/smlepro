# SMLE Pro - Project Context

## Mission
Empower medical students to efficiently prepare for the Saudi Medical Licensing Examination (SMLE) through an AI-powered, interactive quiz platform with instant feedback and personalized learning paths.

## Target Users

### Primary Users
- **Medical students** in Saudi Arabia preparing for SMLE exams
- **Recent medical graduates** needing exam preparation
- **International medical graduates** seeking Saudi licensure

### User Personas

#### 1. Ahmed - The Final Year Student
- **Background**: 5th year medical student at King Saud University
- **Goals**: Pass SMLE on first attempt, track progress across subjects
- **Pain Points**: Limited practice questions, no instant feedback, difficulty identifying weak areas
- **Success Metric**: Complete 1000+ practice questions with 80%+ accuracy

#### 2. Dr. Sarah - The Graduate
- **Background**: Recent graduate, working in hospital while preparing
- **Goals**: Efficient study sessions during limited free time, focus on weak subjects
- **Pain Points**: Time constraints, scattered study materials, no personalized recommendations
- **Success Metric**: 30-minute daily study sessions with measurable progress

#### 3. International Graduate
- **Background**: Foreign medical graduate seeking Saudi licensure
- **Goals**: Understand Saudi-specific medical practices, adapt to local exam format
- **Pain Points**: Cultural differences in medical practice, unfamiliar exam structure
- **Success Metric**: Familiarity with 90%+ of Saudi-specific content

## Problems We're Solving

### 1. Limited Practice Resources
- **Problem**: Students struggle to find comprehensive, up-to-date SMLE practice questions
- **Current State**: Scattered resources, outdated materials, no centralized platform
- **Our Solution**: Curated question bank with regular updates and instant feedback

### 2. No Personalized Learning
- **Problem**: One-size-fits-all study approach doesn't address individual weaknesses
- **Current State**: Students don't know which topics need more focus
- **Our Solution**: Topic-based drills, progress tracking, personalized recommendations

### 3. Delayed Feedback
- **Problem**: Traditional study methods provide feedback too late to correct misunderstandings
- **Current State**: Students complete exams without knowing what they got wrong
- **Our Solution**: Instant feedback with explanations for every question

### 4. Exam Anxiety
- **Problem**: Students lack confidence due to unfamiliarity with exam format
- **Current State**: No simulation of actual exam conditions
- **Our Solution**: Timed quizzes, exam simulation mode, progress visualization

## Jobs to be Done

### Core Jobs
1. **Practice SMLE questions** with immediate feedback
2. **Identify knowledge gaps** across different medical subjects
3. **Track progress** over time to measure improvement
4. **Prepare efficiently** with limited study time

### Secondary Jobs
1. **Access explanations** for correct answers and reasoning
2. **Review mistakes** to avoid repeating them
3. **Simulate exam conditions** with timed quizzes
4. **Get personalized recommendations** for study focus
