SMLE Pro — profile picture files for social networks
====================================================

Why uploads failed before
-------------------------
- TikTok, Instagram, X (Twitter), and YouTube do NOT accept SVG as a profile photo.
- They need a square JPG or PNG (not a wide/banner image).
- Our old smle-pro-logo.png was a wide rectangle — wrong aspect ratio for avatars.

What to use (after deploy, download from your site)
---------------------------------------------------
1. social-profile-1080.jpg  — best default (square, high quality, small file size)
2. social-profile-1080.png  — if a platform insists on PNG
3. social-profile-400.jpg   — if a platform has a low max resolution

Upload the SAME file to TikTok, X, Instagram, and YouTube profile settings.

Regenerate after logo changes
----------------------------
From the project folder:  npm run export-social-pfp
